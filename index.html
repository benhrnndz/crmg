About the CHED TDP Scholarship

The CHED Tulong Dunong Program (TDP) is a scholarship initiative by the Commission on Higher Education of the Republic of the Philippines. It provides financial assistance — including subsistence and book allowances — to qualified college students from low-income families.

Through the endorsement of Congressman Roy Gonzales, representing the Lone District of Santa Rosa, Laguna, eligible residents of Santa Rosa are given priority access to the program for Academic Year 2026–2027.

Program Goals

  • Provide financial assistance to deserving students
  • Increase access to higher education in Santa Rosa
  • Support low-income families send their children to college
  • Promote academic excellence and civic responsibility

Who May Apply

  • Bona fide resident of Santa Rosa, Laguna
  • Enrolled in a CHED-accredited college or university
  • Family income within CHED TDP guidelines
  • Good academic standing (GWA of 2.50 or better)

Office of Congressman Roy Gonzales

Congressman Roy Gonzales represents the Lone District of Santa Rosa, Laguna in the House of Representatives of the Philippines. His office is dedicated to delivering meaningful legislative and social services to every constituent of Santa Rosa.

The CHED TDP scholarship is one of many service initiatives his office facilitates to uplift the quality of life and future prospects of Santa Rosa's youth.
