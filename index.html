CHED TDP Scholarship

The CHED Tulong Dunong Program (TDP) is a scholarship initiative by the Commission on Higher Education. It provides financial assistance — including subsistence and book allowances — to qualified college students from low-income families.

Through the endorsement of Congressman Roy Gonzales, eligible residents of Santa Rosa are given priority access to the program for Academic Year 2026–2027.

Program Goals

  • Provide financial assistance to deserving students
  • Increase access to higher education in Santa Rosa
  • Support low-income families send their children to college
  • Promote academic excellence and civic responsibility

Who May Apply

  • Bona fide resident of Santa Rosa, Laguna
  • Enrolled in a CHED-accredited college or university
  • Family income within CHED TDP guidelines
  • Good academic standing (GWA of 2.50 or better)
